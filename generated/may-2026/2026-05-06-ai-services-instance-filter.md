The AI Services page picks up an instance filter under **More Filters**. Tenants with multiple Snowflake instances can now scope the summary, the total cost card, and the cost graph to a single instance, instead of viewing everything aggregated.

The selection participates in the URL (`?instance=<instance_id>`), so a filtered view is sharable as a link and re-loads in the same scoped state.
