The daily cost alert email and Slack notification pick up a redesigned layout. The HTML email uses a tighter table style, properly inlined chart images, and groups related metrics together so the most useful comparisons (today vs prior period, top warehouses, top teams) are visible without scrolling.

A new query-stats section calls out outlier queries directly in the report, and the Slack version moves to a two-column field layout that scans cleanly on both desktop and mobile.
