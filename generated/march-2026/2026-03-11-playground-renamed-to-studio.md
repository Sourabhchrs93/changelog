The Playground surface across the platform has been renamed to **Studio**. Routes move from `/playground/*` to `/studio/*`, the navigation labels reflect the new name everywhere, and shared/scheduled session links carry the new path.

Old `/playground/*` URLs still resolve, so existing bookmarks and shared session links keep working. The rename is purely a label and routing change — every conversation, schedule, share link, and saved session you had under Playground is the same session, just under its new name.
