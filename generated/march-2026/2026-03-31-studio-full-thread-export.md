Sharing or downloading a Studio session now exports the complete conversation thread, not just the last turn. PDFs and email exports carry every user question and every assistant answer in order, with embedded charts rendered inline so the recipient sees the same content you do.

The reply-by-email and Slack-share paths use the same full-thread export, so a scheduled report sent to the team includes the entire conversation that generated it.
