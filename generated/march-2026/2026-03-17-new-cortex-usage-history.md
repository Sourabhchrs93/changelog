The AI Services cost view picks up two more Cortex surfaces: **Cortex Agent** and **Snowflake Intelligence**. Usage for both is now extracted on the same schedule as Cortex Functions, ingested into the AI Services tables, and exportable to CSV.

Each service gets its own usage table, its own breakdown, and its own place on the AI Services summary graph — so cost questions like "how much did Cortex Agent run for the data-products team last week?" land in the same workflow as the other AI Services.
